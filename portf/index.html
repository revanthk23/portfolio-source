SAMPLE CONSULTATION

Starter plan: a small free-range poultry farm

An example of how a consultation can be laid out. The steps are illustrative and not a promise of results.

1Idea and starting point: confirm the goal, the land, the local market and the birds to start with.
2Setup: plan the shelter, feed and water systems, and basic health and hygiene routines.
3Budget: list the costs for birds, housing, feed, vaccination, equipment and marketing, with a cash reserve for the first months.
4Running and growth: set a weekly routine, track production and sales, and decide when to expand.
5Support: regular check-ins, honest feedback and encouragement through the hard months.

Organic farming

What it is

Growing crops and raising animals without synthetic chemicals, using compost, crop rotation, natural pest control and certified inputs.

Uses

Healthier produce, access to premium and export markets, and better soil over time.

How it makes a profit

Profit depends on certification costs, the price premium your buyers pay, yields during the first years of conversion, and whether you sell directly or through middlemen.

Professional Work

Simulation Route & Scenario Developer

PS Technologies (PST) and Union Pacific Railroad · Nov 2021 – Jun 2023

Built 2D and 3D visual track databases from railway infrastructure specifications, track profiles and grade data, used for locomotive pilot training. Modelled terrain and placed thousands of custom trackside assets, including signalling, switches and yard layouts. Scripted training scenarios with defects, hazards and weather changes, and owned the visual database validation process, working with JIRA to resolve data, texture, physics and rendering issues.

Lead Consultant, ADANI CNG Project

2024

Started as a technical support consultant and was promoted to lead. Onboarded new compressed natural gas stations, stabilised their technical systems, set up support protocols and knowledge-transfer structures for QA and incident teams. Managed a lean team while maintaining service delivery and high client retention.

Agri Business: Livestock Advisor and Farm Owner

Run an agriculture business focused on poultry and dairy, combining traditional, modern and hybrid methods to improve production. Manage automated feeding, water, egg collection and grading, checkweighing and packaging systems, and layer and broiler breeding programmes.

About Me

I am a simulation and scenario developer, a content creator and a business consultant. I hold a B.Tech in Electronics and Instrumentation Engineering from India, where I studied sensors, instruments, automation and industrial safety. My engineering projects ranged from timer and sound-and-light sensor circuits to electromagnetic transducers, including a final project with both medical and mechanical uses.

Beyond the classroom

I organised several fests in my department and volunteered at sports meets and National Credit Corps (NCC) programmes. I took part in national space research activities, and in community programmes on health, rural development, traffic safety, cleanliness and population control, serving people in the areas around my institution. In school I won a national award at a sports meet, and I have competed in art, craft and literature events.
